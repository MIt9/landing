Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. User: “ти вільний змінити все і генерувати за потреби зображення”.

Evidence: personal portfolio for Dmytro Bilukha, senior full-stack engineer / tech lead; React, TypeScript, Node.js; ten years at TEMABIT; real existing metrics, projects, CVs, contact destinations. All factual claims sourced from the original repository page.

## Eight decisions
1. Vibe: sculptural, tactile, decisive, human. References: industrial assembly photography, theatre set construction, annotated museum object labels.
2. Journey: encounter an assembly → inspect two product stories → understand the person and leadership → explore open-source tools → carry chosen work into a conversation.
3. Energy: spatial opening; quiet introduction; peak in a broad project gallery; calmer portrait; compact utility index; decisive contact close.
4. Feeling: intrigue from physical interlocking structure; recognition from clear role; confidence from project evidence; connection from the coach biography; agency from selecting projects; readiness from a composed contact ticket.
5. Signature: visitor assembles a conversation shortlist. Project save buttons update a persistent tray and a contact summary, and selected names become the email subject/body. It is a real local interaction, no invented telemetry.
6. Range: expressive industrial gallery, charcoal and vermilion, warm-white typography. Not an editorial CV spread.
7. Distinct rooms, not a continuous flight. Natural flow plus one sticky gallery encounter. Mobile has its own vertical arrangement.
8. Assets: generated hero sculpture with transparent background; CSS room planes and precision lines; real supplied portrait and existing demo thumbnails. No generated likeness or fake product screenshots. $3 maximum external generation budget. Prefer built-in imagegen, no KIE spend without known pricing.

## Grammar / gate
Custom assembly gallery. Persistent bottom navigation is a real room index plus shortlist count. Hero is a sculptural encounter with title, not a split portrait. Sequence has six rooms and two substantial product exhibits. Close is a conversation ticket populated by selections, not a contact masthead.
Bans: fake dashboard/terminal, decorative metrics, continuous camera flight, full-frame dark overlays, section counters, magnetic controls, repeated equal cards.
Against bilukha-fieldnotes: grammar, bottom-dock nav, sculptural hero, six-room gallery sequence, populated contact ticket and shortlist interaction all differ (6/6). The historical row is retained.
Stock grammar choices: gallery is closest but its object-one-only hero excludes a clear personal introduction; filmic removes jump navigation; editorial disallows image-led arrival; live surface would misrepresent the offering; continuous world is needlessly expensive; poster loses physical evidence; split stage invents an opposition; cutlist is too hurried for hiring decisions.

## Layer contract
Far: charcoal architectural lines, stationary, no photographic duplicates.
Mid: warm-white oversized HTML wordmark behind sculpture, slower displacement.
Subject: generated orange interlocking machined structure, genuine alpha, isolated suspended object, scroll rotation/translation with restrained pointer response.
Near: HTML material labels and lower framing lip, opposite small displacement. Never covers role or controls.
Typography: name and role remain readable at every position. Phone places title above object; reduced motion retains static overlap and depth.
Opening: coherent whole sculpture. Midpoint: object approaches, labels separate in depth. Exit: sculpture recedes as real work enters.
